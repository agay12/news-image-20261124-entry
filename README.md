# One image: the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026

The make.fun `news-image-20261124` deliverable as one committed image: an editorial
six-card poster summing up the day's world news, 1024x1024.

Six verified stories, each first reported 23-24 November 2026:

- COP31 in Antalya collapses with no deal (EU + 130 parties reject a text omitting fossil fuels)
- AI sell-off spreads worldwide: S&P 500 -3.7%, Nikkei -5.06%, Hong Kong at a 19-month low
- US Treasury demands answers from Nvidia over chip sales to China; Jensen Huang resigned
- Russian corvette *Merkury* sunk at Benghazi by a suspected drone attack; Dymer strike kills 10
- EU and Indonesia sign a free trade and investment pact covering 730 million people
- House Democrats win 258 seats, their best general election in decades

Sources: AP, 10 Things, One Turn, Zanders, Morningstar.

## Entrypoint

`main.py` writes the poster to the output directory (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `/output`),
creating the directory first, as `entry.png`.
